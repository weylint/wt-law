The Constitution

of The Federation of White Tiger

# § 1 The Federation

(1) <sup>1</sup>The Federation of White Tiger is a constitutional, federal monarchy composed of states and towns, which can both be founded freely by a sufficient number of citizens. <sup>2</sup>The Federation and its states and towns are administrative units in descending hierarchical order. <sup>3</sup>Towns may become members of states by elective accession or mandatory annexation. <sup>4</sup>The Federation’s sole legal tender is the currency “Pound”.

(2) <sup>1</sup>The Federation’s national objectives are societal and technological advancement; the upholding of its constitutional, democratic and social values; the preservation of nature and the protection of the planet and all its inhabitants from natural disasters.

(3) <sup>1</sup>The elections for all federal public offices shall be held on Days 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29. <sup>2</sup>Retired officials continue to serve in acting capacity until their successor takes office.

# § 2 Basic Rights

(1) <sup>1</sup>All citizens of the federation are equal before the law. <sup>2</sup>They enjoy freedom of faith, conscience and ideological confession as well as freedom of movement and residence. <sup>3</sup>They have the right to - in the boundaries of law - assemble peacefully, freely choose their occupation, freely express their opinion and form societies and other associations.

<sup>4</sup>They also have the right to file and participate in binding referendums and court cases.

(2) <sup>1</sup>Minorities and ethical playstyles may not be discriminated against, legislation that would disproportionately affect them must provide for appropriate hardship provisions.

<sup>2</sup>Settlements must treat all their residents equally and may not limit or tax access to and usage of public property based on citizenship or residence. <sup>3</sup>They may however levy reasonable fees or taxes from non-residents for access to and usage of public services.

(3) <sup>1</sup>Private property shall be guaranteed, but it entails obligations and its use shall also serve the public good. <sup>2</sup>Expropriation is only permissible for the public good and in conjunction with equitable compensation. <sup>3</sup>The Federal Bank may hold bank deposits of abandoned citizens of the federation in trust until their return.

(4) <sup>1</sup>Taxes on income may exclusively be levied by the Federation in an equitable, progressive form and not surpass 50% of the taxable income. <sup>2</sup>Taxes on the trade of goods may exclusively be levied by states and towns and not surpass 5% of the transaction value per administrative unit; this does not apply to steering taxes.<sup>3</sup>Citizens that haven’t learned a specialty may not be subject to taxes on the trade of goods at stores they don’t own and expenses related to such trades must be considered fully when determining their taxable income.  
(5) <sup>1</sup>Government agencies are required to purchase all goods necessary to fulfil their duties passively at official stores and may only actively purchase goods at private stores in cases of justified necessity. <sup>2</sup>Unless explicitly stated otherwise, they may not accept donations. <sup>3</sup>Unauthorized acceptance of donations constitutes malfeasance.

# § 3 States and Towns

(1) <sup>1</sup>States and Towns enjoy autonomy for their finances, governance and legislation.  <sup>2</sup>Their constitution must not assign any powers directly to specific citizens and must establish a Governor respectively Mayor as Head of Government that is electable by all their non-abandoned citizens from all their active citizens. <sup>3</sup>The legislature of a state or town is performed by its Head of Government, except for if its constitution determines it to instead be performed by specific bodies to which no executive powers may then be delegated. <sup>4</sup>Their citizens have the right to demand an election to be held for any office they are eligible to vote for if that office is vacant or the last election for it finished 6 or more days ago. <sup>5</sup>Their non-abandoned citizens additionally have the right to at any time start or vote in a referendum to amend its constitution, the result being determined by simple majority after 24 hours.

(2) <sup>1</sup>States and Towns develop their territory, primarily by establishing infrastructure and public services and support the Federation with its duties. <sup>2</sup>Their executive may appoint Civil Servants serving at its pleasure or instruct contractors to assist them in doing so, in exchange for adequate compensation. <sup>3</sup>They may assign property and zone their territory at their political discretion, exercised in a reasonable way free of arbitrariness.

(3) <sup>1</sup>States are entitled to provide Lower and Higher Education through College Professors in State Colleges. <sup>2</sup>Towns are entitled to provide Lower Education through School Teachers in School Houses. <sup>3</sup>College Professors and School Teachers are appointed by and serving at the pleasure of their local governments and confirmed as Federal Civil Servants by the Minister of Education.  
(4) <sup>1</sup>The State of Unica serving as the Capital City of the Federation additionally has the obligation to provide a central living, meeting and trade hub as well as a place to house the buildings for the constitutional institutions and federal public services. <sup>2</sup>It is entitled to reasonable financial support from the Federation to fulfil these additional obligations.

# § 4 The Parliament

(1) <sup>1</sup>Parliament consists of The House of Commons as its lower and The House of Lords as its upper chamber. <sup>2</sup>The House of Commons is composed of the Prime Minister and 4 Members of Parliament that shall be elected by all non-abandoned citizens of the Federation from the active citizens of the same in a single election for a legislative period of 7 days with no term limit applying, vacancies shall be filled until the next election by eligible candidates defeated in the last election in order of the election result. <sup>3</sup>The House of Lords is composed of all citizens entitled pursuant to §5 (2) Nr. 2.

(2) <sup>1</sup>The House of Commons exercises the fiscal sovereignty of the Federation, providing the Federal Government with budgets and regulations on their use and is responsible to enact legislation in accordance with constitutional law to fulfil the national objectives and to provide policy for a healthy cohabitation of the society.<sup>2</sup>Its members shall be paid a wage according to the federal salary scale and enjoy immunity from criminal prosecution during their tenure that can be lifted by a simple majority of its members. <sup>3</sup>If it is equally divided, the Prime Minister casts the tie-breaking vote. <sup>4</sup>It may conduct public investigations and for that purpose has the authority to require production of evidence and testimony. <sup>5</sup>It may authorize government agencies to act as a market competitor where that is reasonable to restore or preserve economic stability.

(3) <sup>1</sup>The House of Lords advises the House of Commons on legislation. <sup>2</sup>Federal legislation incurring debt to the Federal Bank requires its consent to be valid, which may not be granted if the resulting debt would exceed 10% of the currency in circulation that was not created by debts. <sup>3</sup>Federal legislation explicitly affecting specific states or their territory without their explicit consent requires the approval of the House of Lords to be valid. <sup>4</sup>Its members may not execute their duties while they hold any other federal office.

# § 5 The Crown

(1) <sup>1</sup>The Crown is the embodiment of the state and its authority, all federal sovereign acts hence happen in its name. <sup>2</sup>It is represented by the Community Manager of Eco acting as the Monarch, who enjoys sovereign immunity. <sup>3</sup>The Monarch serves as Head of State of the Federation and is responsible for protecting and preserving this Federal Constitution. <sup>4</sup>Property of the Crown and the Monarch is exempted from all taxation. <sup>5</sup>All powers of the Monarch may, in his absence or on his express instruction, be executed jointly by at least two impartial Game Masters acting as Counsellors of State.

(2) <sup>1</sup>The Monarch grants or withholds royal assent and executes the royal prerogative of mercy. <sup>2</sup>He also creates, grants and revokes honours that may entitle its holders to a membership in the House of Lords.

(3) <sup>1</sup>The Monarch may, on request of the Prime Minister or if a vacancy of that office cannot be filled, dissolve the House of Commons. <sup>2</sup>Once the dissolution is declared, the current legislative period ends prematurely and an immediate special election is held. <sup>3</sup>The legislative period created by that election only lasts until the next general election.

# § 6 The Federal Government

(1) <sup>1</sup>The Federal Government consists of the Prime Minister; the Federal Ministers of Culture, Education and Infrastructure as well as any number of State Secretaries managing government departments not established in this constitution, which shall all be paid a salary according to the federal salary scale. <sup>2</sup>The Prime Minister shall be elected by all non-abandoned citizens of the Federation from the active citizens of the same for the next legislative period with no term limit applying. <sup>3</sup>The Federal Ministers and State Secretaries are appointed by the Prime Minister from the active citizens of the Federation for the current legislative period with no term limit applying and serve at the pleasure of the Prime Minister. <sup>4</sup>For the first 24 hours after their appointment, they serve in acting capacity. <sup>5</sup>All members of the Federal Government may adjust the coverage of established districts related to their duties in accordance with their stated purpose.

(2) ¹The Prime Minister is the head of government, determines the guidelines of government policy and has the authority to issue government bonds at his own discretion. <sup>2</sup>If the office becomes vacant, the first willing, incumbent Minister in order of department creation becomes Prime Minister for the remainder of the legislative period.

(3) <sup>1</sup>The Minister of Education is responsible for progressing the Federation through the [research schedule](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i0BbrYrKPfL-wFLYUdctm_PwO3_HrJSB31sPK0fA86U/view?tab%3Dt.0%23heading%3Dh.cujrz0xx9vy7&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1779011919125844&usg=AOvVaw1cw_JykWp36hjJUs2FuBJq) by crafting “Skill Books” and for providing education of all levels by crafting "Skill Scrolls" and ensuring their equitable distribution by the Federal University and the States and Towns. <sup>2</sup>The education system should be managed economically, but it is entitled to receive funding from Parliament as necessary for smooth operations.

(4) <sup>1</sup>The Minister of Infrastructure oversees and supports the development of the global electricity network, the Federal Highways and the Waterways, including public ports. <sup>2</sup>He advises the house on related budget plans and construction programs, determines the priority of construction projects and works with all local authorities to ensure their obligations in regard to local infrastructure are fulfilled and supported where necessary.

(5) <sup>1</sup>The Minister of Culture is responsible for the cultural development of the Federation as a whole, collaborating closely with its states and towns. <sup>2</sup>He encourages and supports culturally valuable buildings, events and other projects and plans and executes such on behalf of the Federation. <sup>3</sup>Additionally to his budget he may accept donations to a governmental bank account, which may only be used to fulfill the duties of his office.

(6) <sup>1</sup>The Federal Government as a whole ensures the execution of federal legislation. <sup>2</sup>It develops the Federation, primarily by establishing infrastructure and public services in all of its territory. <sup>3</sup>It may appoint Federal Civil Servants serving at its pleasure or instruct adequately compensated Federal Contractors to assist it in doing so.

# § 7 The Judiciary

(1) <sup>1</sup>The Judiciary is composed of Magistrate Judges, which are Federal Civil Servants appointed by and serving at the pleasure of the Monarch. <sup>2</sup>It conducts court cases, impeachments and referendums and determines the procedural rules for each of them. <sup>3</sup>Magistrate Judges may not exercise their duties while they hold any other public office.

(2) <sup>1</sup>If a decision depends on the interpretation of constitutional law, the court must refer all relevant questions to the Monarch, whose interpretation is binding for the court.

# § 8 Sovereign Acts

(1) <sup>1</sup>Legislation must be sufficiently determined, equitable and proportionate as well as suitable and necessary to reach the goal that is pursued with it. <sup>2</sup>Unless provided otherwise, its enforcement is incumbent upon the lowest competent administrative unit. <sup>3</sup>If that is incapable or unwilling to fulfil this duty, any superior competent administrative unit can execute substitute performance or request royal assent to issue binding orders, withhold financial resources or temporarily assume control over the administrative units' government to enforce compliance. <sup>4</sup>Federal legislation - including such passed through a referendum - requires royal assent to become valid; solely mechanical laws are provisionally enforceable and become valid automatically if royal assent is not withheld within 24 hours after its passing. <sup>5</sup>If legislation from different administrative units is conflicting, the legislation of the superior one takes precedence unless deviation is explicitly permitted.

(2) <sup>1</sup>Administrative acts are the imposition of any sovereign measure by the executive of a competent administrative unit to further a legitimate end and must be suitable, necessary and proportionate. <sup>2</sup>They must be notified to those concerned in a manner that ensures its reception and provide information about the measure imposed, the date it was imposed, its reasons, any costs that must be borne by the recipient and an instruction on the right of appeal within three days of their issuance. <sup>3</sup>Measures involving access to or alteration of real estate and confiscation or expropriation of property require prior judicial approval, unless the measure is taken due to imminent danger or as a distress for unpaid debt or fines. <sup>4</sup>Administrative fines may be imposed for violation of laws that provide this option within the range determined by them. <sup>5</sup>They may be doubled if the violation is either intentional, persistent after 24 hours or a repeat offence and the then resulting fine must be doubled if the offender is a legal entity.

(3) <sup>1</sup>Justice is administered according to procedures resembling an inquisitorial, civic law legal system. <sup>2</sup>The Judiciary may order any suitable and proportionate measure necessary to redress ascertained injustice provided that applied law does not provide an exhaustive list of legal consequences or requires specific ones. <sup>3</sup>In criminal cases they may impose fines measured in daily rates multiplicated by half of the applicable average daily income, the temporary or permanent forfeiture of rights and temporary or permanent imprisonment depending on which punishment the violated law specifies and with all these legal consequences except permanent imprisonment being issuable in a suspended form and intended to regularly be issued in such form for a first-time offence. <sup>4</sup>For minor offences they may issue a warning instead of a penalty or convert a fine to community service of one hour per incurred daily rate if the convict requests that. <sup>5</sup>Injunctions are reserved for the judiciary and may not be used by anyone not part of it.

# § 9 Special Regulations

(1) <sup>1</sup>Deviating from §2 (4) taxes on income may have brackets with a taxation exceeding 50% of the taxable income, as long as those brackets only target players with an income value that compared to the income value of most other taxed entities is a significant outlier. <sup>2</sup>Taxation resulting from documented components of a tax on income that serve the purpose of altering the taxation based on vertical integration does not count against the limitation.
