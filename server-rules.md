# § 1 GENERAL

(1) <sup>1</sup>This official server features a simulated social system based on the [constitution document](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1Wlbsfthj62koGAXfGN8Fz2SeNWyUGDxlKFA749OZiP0/edit&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1788388011612153&usg=AOvVaw1KKQqMxQQLrSHcfH5RMoqu) establishing a global “Federation of White Tiger” and a [federal law document](https://www.google.com/url?q=https://docs.google.com/document/u/0/d/1GsXK8j9ivQAArb1RPeeQJJOcstsp1eJL2OdOhrkvqvc/edit&sa=D&source=editors&ust=1788388011612462&usg=AOvVaw0q8GEQRoMOogkc1GsAKwgP) containing written laws as part of its concept aiming to introduce a meta-level to Eco. <sup>2</sup>Laws created by game mechanics are binding based on their written description, even if they don’t have automated effects or when those are not working correctly. <sup>3</sup>Laws with a [SC] prefix implement the server concept and are immutable for players, while laws with a [C] prefix extend or implement the constitution document and, like itself, during a running cycle may only be amended by a constitutional referendum achieving a two thirds majority and a turnout of at least 7,5 % of all players and 30 % of active players.

(2) <sup>1</sup>Disrespecting, insulting or provoking other players may incur a warning and if necessary a temporary mute, in severe cases including verbal bullying and harassment at least a warning and up to a temporary ban. <sup>2</sup>Publishing inappropriate or unlawful content incurs its removal and at least a warning and up to a permanent ban. <sup>3</sup>Discrimination, hate speech, racism, sexism and similar zero tolerance offences violating Eco’s Terms of Service incur at least a temporary and up to a permanent ban.

(3) <sup>1</sup>Playing on this server with more than one account is prohibited and incurs a temporary ban for the main account and a permanent ban for every other account used. <sup>2</sup>Logging into this server for the main purpose of providing advantages to someone else while not actively conducting significant gameplay oneself as well as condoning or encouraging such behaviour as its beneficiary is prohibited and incurs a temporary ban.

<sup>3</sup>This also applies when the beneficiary is a / one’s own company or a government body.

(4) <sup>1</sup>Game Masters are volunteers tasked with addressing emerging issues, assisting government bodies with their duties, helping players with questions and problems and enforcing the server rules. <sup>2</sup>As long as a constitutional office required for orderly gameplay is unable or unwilling to fulfil its duties, they may act in its capacity. <sup>3</sup>They may take any suitable, necessary and proportionate action to fulfil their duties, including but not limited to reverting unlawful game states caused by rule violations and issuing punishments for rule violations as stated in each rule - in especially minor or major cases they can deviate from the stated punishments at their own discretion. <sup>4</sup>Disobedience to their instructions or unsolicitedly discussing with or being disrespectful to them, especially after or during an administrative action, incurs a warning. <sup>5</sup>Their decisions can be appealed to the Community Manager of Eco.

(5) <sup>1</sup>Warnings are permanent, but may become inactive due to expiration or good conduct, depending on the offence they were issued for. <sup>2</sup>Accumulating multiple active warnings usually entails a temporary ban at three and a permanent ban at five warnings. <sup>3</sup>Temporary bans are typically issued for the remainder of the current cycle, but in special cases may also be issued with a different, shorter or longer period of validity. <sup>4</sup>Bans and warnings issued for violations of these rules only apply to this official server.

§ 2 GAME MECHANICS

(1) <sup>1</sup>Abusing game mechanics, including those specific to this server, in a way not intended by Strange Loop Games to disturb other players ("Griefing'') or to gain an advantage for oneself or a third party over other players (''Exploiting") is prohibited and incurs a warning, in severe cases at least a temporary and up to a permanent ban. <sup>2</sup>Placing crops or oil pumps in vertical layers and using the storage ui to move items over notable horizontal distances beyond the constraints of a single deed or district that is used for personal or public operations are examples of what is considered an exploit.

(2) <sup>1</sup>Players may not collaborate by sharing deeds, property or workforce unless being members of the same company, except for when the collaboration has been approved by Game Masters and follows all imposed conditions or takes place as part of a governmental project that meets all constitutional requirements. <sup>2</sup>Companies may only have a single member holding a state or federal office. <sup>3</sup>Governmental and private property must be strictly separated at all times. <sup>4</sup>Business involving access to private property must be verifiably recorded through contracts. <sup>5</sup>Violation of this rule may incur a warning or, in severe cases, a temporary ban.

(3) <sup>1</sup>Players may not give up and unclaim used deeds. <sup>2</sup>They may also not gift or donate any property when considering or already having decided to leave the server. <sup>3</sup>Violation of this rule may incur a warning or, in severe cases, a temporary ban.

(4) <sup>1</sup>Reputation awarded to deeds, pictures or players manually must comprehensively reflect one’s actual personal opinion about them. <sup>2</sup>It may not be traded, but governments may incentivize players to use their reputation allocation on deeds and pictures and they may also give reasonable reputation to players for (un)desirable behaviour through civics. <sup>3</sup>It may not be abused by attaching defamatory comments to it, by using it as any kind of leverage or for unreasonable organised retaliation. <sup>4</sup>Asking for or providing reputation to a deed in relation to a dinner party does not violate this rule. <sup>5</sup>Violation of this rule may incur a warning and will entail the removal of infringing reputation.
